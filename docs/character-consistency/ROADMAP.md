# Goals, hypotheses and waypoints

All performance thresholds below are **proposed acceptance targets**, not measured model performance. Change a threshold only through a recorded decision before evaluating a fresh held-out run; do not lower it after seeing a failure and relabel the experiment successful.

## Ultimate goal

From an approved original-character specification, produce a versioned pack with four consistent views, four expression portraits, a deliberately simplified miniature representation, editable source, an authored idle clip, common-anchor atlas metadata and actual target-engine playback. Repeating a layout/export operation should cost zero neural calls. Agents may propose and execute authorized bounded work; they must stop on uncertain submissions, exhausted budgets or unresolved critical review rather than hiding failure.

The final automation trial uses two independently reviewed production packs plus a held-out identity/costume case. This establishes a useful bounded capability, not universal autonomous art production.

W2 has **no minimum beauty/pass percentage**: its purpose is to learn whether to continue. Do not force a success label. W3's proposed 24-attempt ceiling is an effort target; failure should produce the best accepted intermediate and a defect report. A broader 48-attempt multi-route research study is an alternative experiment, not added credit for W3.

For W5, proposed productivity targets are at least 75% first-pass selection and at least 30% reduction in median hands-on cleanup compared with the recorded manual baseline on the same brief class. Report all denominators and elapsed effort, not only successful attempts. Tiny pilot samples cannot establish reliable population rates. Do not demand unattended owner approval; the automation boundary includes a deliberate approval checkpoint.

## Baselines and metrics

B0 is deterministic extraction/assembly from supplied images: actual source identity, pixel preservation and layout repeatability, **not model-quality evidence**. B1 is the exact currently installed Qwen reference path. B2 is the exact current Klein edit path. Model variants, quantization, dimensions, reference processing and runtime pins are part of baseline identity. A changed baseline starts a new plan; retain the previous evidence.

Track attempts used/remaining, planned cases, completed attempts, selected cases, owner-accepted cases, first-pass selection, planned-case coverage, generation time, cleanup time, failure classes and useful outputs. `seconds_per_selected_case` includes rejected/warmup/repair time when all measurements exist. Null means unknown, not zero. Report engine acceptance and rights separately.

## Sequencing and human decisions

Do W0/W1 now, then #64 with one accepted costume. Complete W3 before training a character LoRA. Choose authored 2D or 3D representation at W4 based on motion reuse, not model hype. Build the live coordinator and critic calibration alongside measured needs, not as prerequisites for every manual experiment.

The draft supplied-character canon is a starting proposal. Resolve hidden back construction and the original-character target with the owner without reopening decisions already recorded in HUMAN_TODO.md. Existing optional pixel-look/product/culling choices and Anima q-3 remain open. Agents can prepare alternatives and recommend; they cannot silently mark these decisions done.
