## 5. Identity contract and hard stop rules

Bind the policy to the immutable concept receipt before scoring candidates.
Candidate input cannot choose easier crops, waive features, change tolerances,
remove views or select a different root. Every required observation must be
`pass`; `fail`, `not_visible`, `uncertain`, missing evidence and stale ancestry
block the dependent transition. A high mean cannot cancel a failed critical cue.

The frozen policy should explicitly separate:

- **Identity:** face shape/landmarks, eye and hair construction, proportions,
  silhouette, distinctive accessories and asymmetries, taken from the exact A2.
- **Declared variation:** expression muscles, pose, camera and the specifically
  approved outfit region. Changing an expression does not permit a different eye
  design; changing an outfit does not permit a different body.
- **View context:** camera/scale, visibility, expected foreground coverage,
  cropping and lighting. Side/back views have different observable cues.
- **Evidence provenance:** measured pixels/geometry versus human annotations,
  exact media subject hashes, reviewer record and current acceptance scope.
- **Unknown construction:** mark unseen details as proposals until reviewed.
  Mirroring an asymmetry or filling an occluded area is not a neutral operation.

No real A2 pixels or held-out owner-labelled calibration set were supplied here.
Consequently, there is **no calibrated Sable perceptual threshold** in this plan.
Synthetic tests and manually supplied descriptors verify software rules; they do
not establish facial recognition accuracy. Pixel/feature agreement remains a
diagnostic proxy, and owner visual acceptance remains independent.

Recheck actual current review, selected heads and byte subjects immediately before
queue admission and publication. Replacing/revoking the concept invalidates all
dependent heads; replacing a selected intermediate invalidates its descendants.
Retain stale records for history. A bad unselected alternative does not poison a
separately accepted branch.

An uncertain submission blocks further attempts until its retained job/prompt
history is reconciled. Missing media does not mean no attempt occurred. Keep the
same known prompt ID while observing it; do not submit again to get a cleaner
receipt. Draft export, native import and release have separate gates.

## 7. Safe route and one bold experiment

**Safe route:** recover A2, preserve its authored source, take native matched views,
perform scoped repairs, and use the existing rig/look/native import path that
matches its actual skeleton. Its first success criterion is **one exact accepted
source mapping**, followed by eight individually admissible receipts. A separate
native import/playback receipt is still required. This path avoids spending neural
attempts to recreate already accepted work.

**Bold experiment, planned only:** test a candidate sheet and derived mesh as a
closed inspection loop. After authorization and rights/source readiness, one
TripleView proposal uses the exact accepted image. If its views pass the declared
human and proxy checks, a separately authorized mesh contingency may produce a
sculpt base. Render that base through frozen front/left/right/back and near-eye
cameras and compare **each** view directly to the accepted concept and accepted
supplemental construction. Compare the native-source route alongside it.

The purpose is to find contradictions early: eye/proportion drift, flipped
asymmetry, invented coat backs, fused fingers and texture-only details that fail
in geometry. The loop never promotes a generated view into the root concept and
never accepts itself merely because its own generated views agree.

**Kill gates:** any required identity mismatch; invented hidden construction
without scoped acceptance; missing current authority; unresolved TripleView or
DINO-derived source/terms; missing exact local patch/model; an OOM/device fault;
the route's deformation failure; or its attempt/time cap. Stop the experimental
branch and retain the safe intermediate. This uses existing catalogued candidates;
the newer DynamicCharacterSheet/Krea branch discovered upstream is not added to
the portfolio or installed. Its extra dependencies and admitted inter-view
inconsistency do not resolve the current source-mapping gap. [W7]
