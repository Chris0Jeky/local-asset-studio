# Convergence programme

## Objective

Convert the repository's strong infrastructure into a small number of owner-accepted, reusable creative workflows before expanding the mainline scope.

Convergence does not mean freezing research or removing advanced capability. It means that new work must either:

- complete the accepted-asset loop;
- remove a measured blocker or expensive failure;
- consolidate a proven repeated pattern;
- unlock the next bounded vertical slice.

## Current starting point

At this bundle's snapshot, all previously active PRs had been reconciled and `main` was clean of open pull requests. This is the ideal point to reset delivery around outcomes rather than restart many parallel branches.

## WIP policy

Initial limit from #315:

- maximum **three independent implementation lines**;
- plus **one declared stack** with merge order;
- one finite owner-run neural/native acceptance lane may operate separately.

Before opening another line, an active line must merge, close, be parked with a handoff, or receive an explicit owner override.

## Integration rules

1. Reconcile live issue and PR state before implementation.
2. Prefer the smallest slice that produces user-visible proof.
3. Use stacked PRs only where source dependency is real; declare parent, merge order and retarget plan.
4. Do not repeatedly merge moving `main` into every long-lived branch; keep branches short or use a deliberate integration checkpoint.
5. Run focused causal tests during development, shared contracts for changed owners, and broad integration at merge.
6. Keep neural/native/owner acceptance separate from routine CI.
7. Put detailed hashes and raw logs in reconciliation documents or artifacts, not all in the PR first screen.
8. Open follow-up issues only for reproduced gaps with distinct ownership.

## Stop conditions

Stop a route and report when:

- the finite attempt or repair budget is exhausted;
- a submission outcome is uncertain;
- the same failure repeats without a changed hypothesis;
- resource admission or runtime identity is unknown;
- a source/revision changed after review;
- the work requires a new subsystem not justified by the current vertical slice;
- the user-visible result remains worse than direct ComfyUI/native-tool use.

Stopping with exact evidence is a successful outcome when it prevents unbounded effort.

## Work deliberately not started during convergence

- additional model families without a specific blocked task;
- generic autonomous critic/promotion;
- broad voice/music/NLE integration;
- framework rewrites;
- a second asset store, queue or model registry;
- unrestricted authored graph execution;
- automatic runtime/package mutation;
- test-count expansion as an objective.

## Exit criteria

The convergence phase can be considered successful when:

- the owner has a small accepted reusable asset pack produced through the Studio;
- the same route is repeatable without hidden agent state;
- review naturally leads to reuse, repair or export;
- predictable resource/validation blockers refuse early;
- one saved authored workflow can execute through the trusted runtime boundary;
- one Repair Studio R1 result is accepted;
- the active queue and status documents remain understandable without reconstructing dozens of PRs.

These are evidence gates, not calendar commitments.
