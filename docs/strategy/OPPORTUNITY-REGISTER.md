## Additional ideas worth retaining

### Asset inbox and organisation engine

Use current review states plus grouping, reason tags, lineage and continuation suggestions. Automation may propose groups and next actions, but must not promote creative quality automatically.

### Route explanation as evidence

Every proposed route should answer:

- why this model/recipe fits the task;
- which references are used and how;
- measured/estimated/unknown cost;
- known limitations;
- what changes if the owner chooses an alternative.

### Failure-guided next action

After a failure, propose a materially different intervention or stop. Do not translate “needs work” into a stronger denoise value without evidence.

### Accepted-asset portfolio

A small curated owner portfolio is more useful than a large raw gallery. Each item should show exact route, source lineage, why it was accepted, a failure example and possible continuations.

### Private/public evidence split

Public documentation should retain reproducible structure and sanitised identities. Raw workstation paths, private media and detailed process receipts belong in ignored local evidence unless they materially help maintainers.

### First-class cost classes

Expose rough route classes such as seconds, under five minutes, ten-plus minutes and unknown. Do not promise exact time from stale measurements, but prevent accidental selection of a twelve-minute route as if it were interactive.
