# Night Shift quality study 02

The version 1 files in the parent directory remain unchanged early studies. The owner found them below the requested pictorial illustration bar. This iteration targets those specific weaknesses; it is another visual checkpoint, not finished or accepted production art.

## Workshop

![Full-size Chromium rendering](night-shift-workshop-v2-preview.png)

`night-shift-workshop-v2.svg` (1600×900) has 21 editable named layers. A coherent desk plane, cabinet faces and rounded CRT replace the flat blockout. Keycaps and paper use the desk perspective; fabric seams and worn wood follow their materials. Layered window reveals, metal edges, glazing, paired rain refraction and city/rail structures separate foreground and distance. Warm lamp falloff, cyan bounce and contact shadows establish the light hierarchy while the left wall stays quiet.

Native gradients and native SVG Gaussian-blur filters soften light and shadows. Every shape remains an editable path/primitive; there is no embedded image, raster tracing, external content or script. The static master is complete. Optional restrained motion belongs only to `review.html` and pauses with reduced motion, hidden documents and actual offscreen observation.

## Pixel study

![Integer 3× rendering of the native 384×216 grid](night-shift-pixel-v2-preview.png)

`night-shift-pixel-v2.svg` uses 13 named layers on a 384×216 native grid. It replaces arbitrary wall dots with structured facade banks, CRT case/phosphor shading, keycap clusters, restrained grain and localized reflection clusters. Selective two-colour dithering belongs only to the light transition. It is a deliberate pixel interpretation rather than a texture overlay on the smooth vector scene.

## Comparison and limits

`review.html` places preserved version 1 and version 2 side by side, with the unchanged accepted MA evidence panel. Actual Chromium renders were inspected at full and 390px phone widths; the comparison/lifecycle probe passes 36 checks across LAS and Alibi. These counts do not establish artistic acceptance.

The accepted evidence panel is 480×270. Its native pixels and a scaled full/phone comparison were inspected, but the full 3840×2160 MA master is still blocked by the supplied Windows Library xattr helper. The original's irregular painted surface remains richer than these native vector studies. Final full-resolution comparison and artistic judgement remain required.

No runtime activation, budget change, dependency, merge or deployment. The source coverage/selection ledger and explicit deferrals remain unchanged.
