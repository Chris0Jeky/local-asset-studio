# Independent source-art review

## Recommendation

The three sources form a coherent warm walnut, plaster and courtyard environment. The master has a clear drafting-room silhouette, cream paper and a practical lamp. The quiet edit preserves the place and avoids a failure/error mood. The wall removes desk clutter and confines the strongest architectural edge to the right. The hero, poster and card are exact-source crops/encodes, not independent generation.

The hero crop retains the entire desk/stool/basket core and the x≈.78 focus. The poster uses the same fractional 16:9 crop as the scene rendition and its 1280 encode is byte-identical to that scene rendition. The square card prioritizes the desk, lamp and open courtyard; the96px review remains recognizable. Labels appear only on QA contact sheets and annotations, never on source/rendition images.

## Geometric and visual caveats

The requested middle-left 60% is not uniformly quiet: the far-left plant and diagonal wall shadows remain. The conservative reviewed rectangle is normalized x=.16–.48, y=.18–.62. A CSS scrim or solid heading surface is a future suggestion, not tested UI contrast. The wall control-zone proposal is x=.10–.78, y=.10–.80; avoid the right doorway and bottom skirting. 12% overscan is not present around every object, particularly the right basket, foreground plant and rug.

Quiet has the same 1672×941 canvas. 20 hand-selected 41×41 high-pass luminance templates were matched within ±12 px. Every sample peaks at zero integer shift; median NCC = .90569, minimum = .64772. The red/cyan edge overlay supports broad geometry retention. The test samples landmarks, not every pixel, and does not prove subpixel identity, unchanged microtexture or seamless runtime crossfade.

## Scope limits

No obvious watermark, logo, device brand, person or semantic UI text was observed in the source images. The drafting paper contains nonsemantic sketch marks. This is visual inspection, not a legal ownership guarantee or OCR certification.

All 11 encoded renditions decode and fit individual profile budgets. Native-size targets remain deliberately unmet where original pixels cannot supply them. Source/license clearance, actual editor placement, contrast, first-screen aggregate transfer, decoded-memory, loading and accessibility checks remain open. No layers, audio, loops, promotion assets, runtime changes or heavy CI were produced.
