## What the pictures answer

- **(a) and (d): which route is closer to the target.** The Klein route. It kept the composition, the wink, the hat,
  the strapless top, the red-and-black robe with gold trim and the carved throne on all four Klein renders. It also
  gave the high-key finish. The WAI route redesigned the costume on all five renders: a belted black tunic, a starry
  blue hat and heels, with black hair and red eyes. The WAI face and shading are clean, but "costume kept" fails
  there every time. So Klein should keep leading the route, as shipped.
- **Mishima Kurone or Momoko as the WAI look.** A tie. Momoko is a little softer, but both redesign the costume the
  same way, so the choice matters less than the route.
- **The finish sentence.** The research render that names the colours (`next-k4-text2-s6`: purple hair, blue eyes)
  is the only one that kept the source's hair and eye colours. The shipped run turned the hair black and the open
  eye violet. A likely cause: the appended source description is the source's *prompt* ("long dark hair"), not what
  the source picture shows (purple hair), and Klein follows the words. Suggested fix: when the source's own words
  and pixels disagree, name the colours seen in the picture. This is a hypothesis from one comparison; it is untested.
- **A repeat defect to fix.** Every Klein restyle drew the raised leg's foot as a toe-less, wrapped stocking tip.
  The source wears a stocking on that leg, and the restyle removed the stocking but kept its shape. A "bare foot"
  phrase or a foot inpaint should fix it.
- **(b) Should the style board stay required?** The one board-on render (0.3) tinted the hair and costume, which
  supports shipping it off. Whether it should also become optional (#351) is a UI question the pictures cannot
  answer. The agent's reading: yes, make it optional.
- **(e) Combine: the witch in another picture's pose.** The pose moved on all three renders, with the witch's hat,
  robe and face kept. The two research renders are clean. The Studio proving run has two defects: a torn-paper patch
  on the forward leg and mismatched eye colours, both fixable by reseeding. The README's research found that abstract
  wording failed three times out of three, so the two bracketed fills earn their place.
- **Look from a picture.** Not judged for style. Its look picture (and the Combine's pose picture) is a fan picture of
  a student-canon character, and it was not opened under the overnight adult-only rule. Only what can be judged
  without it is scored.
- **(f)** was already answered by the owner; nothing to add.
