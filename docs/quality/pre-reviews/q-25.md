# q-25 pre-review: the Style + Pose results

Agent pre-review, 23 September 2026. **Agent-judged, not accepted: the owner still decides q-25.**

## How it was judged

- **Blind.** Two fresh subagents (both `claude-opus-5-5`) judged all 55 Style + Pose renders of 14 September blind.
  - Labels were shuffled and metadata stripped.
  - Each judged about half; four pictures were judged by both as a cross-check, with the same verdicts and means
    within 0.34.
  - The review agent then unblinded the labels and spot-checked three pictures.
- **Rubric.** The judges used the [rubric](../JUDGING-RUBRIC.md) with calibration corrections R1–R6. Hands, faces and
  feet were cropped at full resolution.
- **What the judges were given.** The judges saw only words: the subject, the pose, and the target look. The target
  look was taken from the matrix README: a warm red / black / gold palette, glossy cel shading, crisp outlines and a
  diamond trim.
  - They were not given the style board or the pose pictures. The style board includes an excluded source (the
    owner ruling of 23 September covers only derived renders), which no agent opened. The owner ruled on 23 September 2026 that the witch these renders
    show is an adult original, and asked for them to be judged: the ruling is their answer "(a)", relayed by the
    coordinator.
  - So `style` means "fits the described look", and `control` means "follows the described pose".
- **One render skipped.** `Style-Pose/WAI_00008_.png` (sha256 `bdf2fc68…`; the matrix's WAI / no LoRA / beach cell) was
  skipped as adult content by a blind judge; the prompt asked for a clothed witch; files moved local-only in #865.
  It was not described or cropped. That the beach pose can lose requested clothing is itself a finding.

Records, one per judgement, with SHA-256, job and prompt IDs and each configuration:
[q-25.judgements.jsonl](q-25.judgements.jsonl). No crop sheet is committed: most of these renders are pin-up
framings.

**One seed (2026091410) throughout.** Under the protocol, means within 0.3 are ties, and one seed cannot establish
a difference.

## What the pictures answer

- **Is the direction right: WAI or YumeFlux, board averaged at about 0.7, Mishima Kurone 0.8 + Glossy 0.6?**
  - The checkpoint half holds: WAI and YumeFlux are the two best complete-picture checkpoints, tied with each other
    (and with CSTati).
  - The board half holds as a direction: averaging at 0.6 beat averaging at 0.8, and both shipped-default runs are
    among the best renders in the set.
  - The LoRA half is not supported by these pictures. The LoRA sweep tested Mishima 0.7 + Glossy 0.6 (the README's
    best combination), which turned the robe green on WAI (3.67). The shipped default, Mishima 0.8 + Glossy 0.6, appears
    only in the two verify runs (4.00 and 4.17), with no LoRA-off control on the averaged board. Plain YumeFlux (4.17) and YumeFlux + Glossy (4.33) scored at least as well as
    any Mishima cell. Mishima 0.8 alone on WAI (4.00) was the best WAI cell of the LoRA sweep.
  - One seed, so all of this is a lead, not a result.
- **Should the defaults change?** Style weight 0.7 sits between the probes' best (0.6) and the worse 0.8. Nothing here
  argues for moving it. Pose strength 0.9 was never varied, so these pictures cannot answer that half of the question.
- **Recurring defects to expect from this route:**
  - the hand resting on the knee: fused, tangled or nub fingers, in at least eight throne renders;
  - feet cut by the frame edge;
  - colour drift of the robe away from red/black: green, teal, navy or purple on 7 renders;
  - on stronger boards, neon burn.
- **Is it right for the fantasy character pack?** The pictures cannot say. Every render is a seated throne pin-up or a
  beach back view of a witch whose look came from a board, not the pack's character. The pack itself moved to Anima
  look B with reference routes (see q-30).

## Recommendation

- Before adopting Style + Pose for the pack, re-prove it where the pack needs it:
  - the pack's own portrait (look B) as the style board;
  - a standing or action pose;
  - three seeds;
  - pose strength at 0.7, 0.9 and 1.0;
  - a blind judgement.
- Drop the beach pose picture from future matrices unless its prop and clothing problems are the subject of the test.
