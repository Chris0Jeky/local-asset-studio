## What the pictures answer

- **Per image (keeper / needs-work / experiment).** Agent reading: 1, 2, 7 and f1 are the strongest; 3 and 5 are
  superseded by 7; 4 and 6 are failures of their route, useful only as evidence; the g chain renders need a hand
  fix and a finish pass before they could sit beside 1.
- **Which face is the character.** Face 1. It has the defining details (the side-swept fringe, the gold diamond
  earrings, the heavy-lidded calm gaze); face 2 is a rounder, younger-reading face with a bare forehead and no
  visible earrings. Every later route that carried face 1 forward (5, 7, f, g, S) made a more consistent set.
- **Reference route instead of the seed.** Yes, on this evidence. The seed changed the hair in 3; the edits of 1 (5, 7)
  and the replace route (f, S) kept the face, fringe and earrings on 8 of 8 renders. The only identity losses came
  from the depth Combine (6, face blank) and one chain seed (g3, earrings gone).
- **Is the lantern grip in 1 the defect to correct next?** No. At full resolution both hands have five digits and a
  readable pinch on the bail; the earlier "slightly odd" reading does not hold up as a defect. The real flaw in 1 is
  the crop: the lantern's glass and base run off the bottom edge. The hand that does need correcting is the lantern
  hand of 6 and the g chain (scribbled knuckles).
- **(e) Is 5 the expression wanted?** Agent reading: 7 is closer to the brief than 5 (5 overshoots into a grin).
  Both drift the irises from brown to amber-gold, so if eye colour matters, name it in the edit wording.
- **(f) Which replace seed?** The three score the same; by likeness to face 1, **f1** (fringe falling to one side,
  one gold earring at the jaw, half-lidded gaze) > f3 > f2. The Studio recipe at the same seed (S1) matches f1.
- **(g) Pose route, then face route?** It restores the face (3 of 3), but it does not repair the depth render's
  hand or bring back look B's painterly finish. For this pair the direct replace route (f, S: 832×1216 full body
  in, face from the portrait) gives a better picture in one step. The chain is still the route when the pose
  picture is not already a look-B render.
- **Detail Fix (4).** Do not use the combined face-and-hands pass on a face that is already right: here it cost the
  eye colour and both earrings and fixed nothing.
