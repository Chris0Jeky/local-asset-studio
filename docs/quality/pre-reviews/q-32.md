# q-32 pre-review: the 23 September LoRA smoke winners

Agent pre-review, 23 September 2026. **Agent-judged, not accepted: the owner still decides q-32.**
This covers the art-judgement part of q-32: the smoke "winners" on WAI v17, NoobAI XL 1.1 and Pony V6
(`experiments/curated/lora-smoke-20260923/`). Parts (a) to (c) of q-32 (the TextFusion hot path, the eight unpinned
LoRAs, the two recipes with nsfw-flagged adapters) are policy choices, not picture questions, and are left as asked.

## How it was judged

1. A fresh subagent judged all 25 renders blind. It reports the model ID `claude-opus-5-5`.
   - Labels were shuffled within each checkpoint, and metadata and trigger words were stripped from the pictures.
   - It saw each prompt, and knew only that each group held one control plus single-LoRA variants.
   - It cropped both hands and the face of every render.
2. Then the labels were unblinded.
3. The review agent then checked eleven disputed regions itself at full resolution. Each check is recorded as
   `review_audit` in the records.

All 25 pictures show one fully clothed adult elf mage. Records:
[q-32.judgements.jsonl](q-32.judgements.jsonl), with SHA-256 and prompt IDs. Crop sheet:
[`examples/quality/q-32-crops.jpg`](../../../examples/quality/q-32-crops.jpg).

**One seed and one subject.** On the protocol's rule, means within 0.3 are a tie, and one seed cannot establish a
difference. Everything below is a screen for the next test, not a result.

## What the pictures answer

- **Where the blind judge and the smoke README agree:**
  - WAI Masterpiece 0.4 is the best of its group;
  - NoobAI Flat Color 0.85 is the best of its group;
  - Pony Gothic Neon 0.85 is the best of its group;
  - both Stabilizers drift towards semi-real, and the NoobAI one at 1.0 breaks the composition.
- **Where they disagree:**
  - **Hands Pony 0.6 is not a hand fix.** Both strengths removed the control's candle but drew six digit tips on the same
    raised hand (crop 40,420-290,620). The README's "clean open hand" came from a look at the whole frame. The
    `settings-kb.json` entry and the README now say so next to the original claim.
  - **Dramatic Lighting 0.6 changes the subject.** A book replaces the orb. The README noted the book but kept the LoRA.
  - **WAI Stabilizer 0.5 and Velvet 0.6 are not better than the control on this seed.** At 3.8 against the control's 3.6
    they are ties. Velvet is a genuine *style* option (teal line accents), and whether that is wanted is a question of
    taste, not quality.
  - **NoobAI Masterpiece ties the control** (3.4), with a colour fringe the control does not have. This seed shows no
    gain from it.
- **NoobAI framing drift.** The brief asked for "upper body, looking at viewer". The NoobAI control and both
  Masterpiece runs instead drew an over-the-shoulder view from behind. Only Flat Color 0.85 faces the viewer with the
  orb in both hands.
- **Did the blind judge spot the plain control?** It picked it correctly for NoobAI and Pony, but for WAI it picked
  Hands Illu 0.85. That is further evidence that Hands Illu barely changes the picture when the base hands are fine.
- **Adult-only concern (Pony).** The Pony control and the Hands and Dramatic Lighting renders draw a round face with
  very large eyes for "adult woman". The README says so; the blind judge flagged it independently. Under the
  overnight adult-only rule a youthful-looking result is discarded, not repaired. Pony needs stronger adult cues before
  it is used for any character work. Gothic Neon 0.85 is the only Pony render with a clearly adult face.

## Recommendation for the next test (lab)

Re-run the candidates on at least three seeds and the protocol's showcase suite: WAI Masterpiece 0.4 and Micro
Details 0.5, NoobAI Flat Color 0.85 and Pony Gothic Neon 0.85, each against its control. Blind-judge with rubric
corrections R1–R6. Drop Hands Pony from the hand-fix role unless a multi-seed test shows fewer broken hands than the
control.
