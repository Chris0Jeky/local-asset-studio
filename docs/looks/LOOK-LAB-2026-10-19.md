## Result 2: close-up faces and the youthful read

Seven families got the same face brief ("adult woman in her late twenties, black hair, red eyes, pearl earrings"). **Four read youthful** (Pearly mix, Anima base, JANIMA, Anima aesthetic) and were skipped under the standing rule, not scored. One Obsession, WAI and AniFox read as clearly adult. For an adult close-up on Anima use One Obsession, or add mature cues and check the face at full size before keeping it. The Anima adapter `anima-newanimastyle-v1` also made the sorceress read younger (`e1-newanima`): avoid it for adult characters.

## Result 5: Krea 2 and the painted trigger text

The flagship Krea target stack paints its own trigger on the picture in some runs. With `@NJSW33T` at the start of the prompt, TextFusion on, 15 steps `euler_ancestral`, the corner text appeared on seed ...01 (`s1-krea-gguf`) and again when the trigger was moved to the end (`k2-trigger-end`). It did not appear on seeds ...02 and ...03, nor at 8 steps (`euler`) or 4 steps (distill). **Six pictures with the trigger and TextFusion on: 2 with painted text; six pictures without the trigger or without TextFusion: 0.** Dropping the trigger from the prompt, or turning TextFusion off, removed it at the failing seed and the pictures kept the same rich finish. Cheapest fix: leave the trigger out. Timing is dominated by load and the CPU text encode, not sampling: all twelve Krea jobs took 262-331 s (15 steps 262-331 s, 8 steps 287 s, 4 steps 276 s) on this PC.

## Result 6: pose by drawn skeleton (WAI + Xinsir OpenPose)

Ten skeletons (hip-on-hand, sword stance, crossed arms, walk, reach), two proportion sets, strength 0.8-1.0, same character and balcony wording. **Only the simple hand-on-hip pose was followed (control 3-4, at 0.9 and 1.0);** the sword stance, crossed arms, walk and raised arm were ignored (control 1-2) at every strength tried (0.8 and 1.0 on the first skeleton set, 0.9 and 1.0 on the second; all 17 pose pictures judged), and a wide full-body stance became a seated or balustrade-leaning pose. Cause is probably the prompt: the shared "castle balcony, calm smile" wording pulls toward a standing portrait pose. This route is not enough for dynamic poses: use the Klein 9B drawn-skeleton route (5 of 5 followed, Result 6b) when the pose matters, and keep the pose words in the prompt. Result 6b measures the Klein routes on the same skeletons.

## For agents: concrete rules learned

1. Put exclusions in the **negative** for the SDXL checkpoints; the positive "high collar" is not enough. 2. A skipped youthful close-up is a result, not a failure: report it and do not re-run to find one that passes. 3. Anima mixes cost the same 20-25 s at 20 or 40 steps; spend the saved time on seeds, not steps. 4. A sampler change changes the composition, not just texture: it is not an ablation at a fixed seed. 5. Krea timing is load-bound: auditions at 4 steps cost the same as 15. 6. Admission for Krea/Klein-class jobs stayed below 60 % host commit in the Studio's own reading; two Krea jobs peaked at 72 % in flight and finished. 7. The backend recovery relaunch and a killed watcher can leave a receipt in `submission-intent`: recover the job by its ID, never resubmit.
