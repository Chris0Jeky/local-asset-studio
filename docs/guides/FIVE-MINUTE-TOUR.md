# Five-minute tour

A quick pass over the Studio as it is now, replacing the September UX checklist. Do each step once, then say what felt wrong,
slow or confusing, in your own words. Nothing here approves any art or any licence.

Start the Studio with the **Asset Studio** shortcut. It opens on **Make**.

1. **Make.** Keep **Anime character**, press a look and make one picture. Did you know what would happen before you pressed it?
2. **Library.** Open it and go to **Review next**. Judge a few pictures with K, W, X and S. Was it faster than clicking?
3. **Full editor.** Choose *Anima Reference Adapter Turbo*, add one of your own pictures as the reference, describe only the scene
   and generate. It takes about 5 seconds once loaded. Does it still look like your character?
4. **Comparisons.** Plan a comparison from a recipe and read the summary line. Could you tell what it would spend before
   you ran it? Open the finished plan, look at the pictures and use **Pick a winner** or **Choose here**. Is it clear which choice records the comparison's winner?
5. **Workflow lab.** Open any guided workflow. Did it show you the next step without explanation?

Tell an agent your answers. That closes `ux-tour-1101` in `HUMAN_TODO.md`, and each problem you name becomes a tracked issue.
